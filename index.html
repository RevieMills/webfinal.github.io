The Purpose

The Rafflesia is more than just a flower; it is a rare biological masterpiece of our rainforests. Our mission is to bridge the gap between human curiosity and ecological preservation. By documenting these magnificent species and protecting their fragile habitats, we ensure that the 'Corpse Flower' continues to bloom for generations to come. Join us in our journey to explore, understand, and defend the giants of the jungle.






















RAFFLESIA

The Rafflesia is a genus of parasitic flowering plants that holds the record for producing the largest individual flower on Earth. First brought to international attention in 1818 in the rainforests of Sumatra by a local guide working for Dr. Joseph Arnold and Sir Stamford Raffles, this "corpse flower" has become an iconic symbol of Southeast Asian biodiversity.

SPECIES & CONSERVATIION TEAM MAP


The Rafflesia Conservation Map is a digital initiative designed to map the distribution points of Rafflesia arnoldii and its related species throughout the Indonesian rainforests. Through real-time data integration, this map is more than just a location finder; it serves as a critical protection instrument for endemic flora whose status is increasingly threatened.
